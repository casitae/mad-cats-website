Food as a Conversation Between Cultures

DRAG·01 / 05

RESERVE FOOD MENU

We believe food is about bringing people together. Drawing inspiration from the communal food cultures of the Mediterranean, Middle East and South-East Asia, our menu is designed for passing plates, trying a little of everything and discovering flavours together.

We wanted to create a space that feels familiar: a great record, a few shared dishes and another round. Whether you're here for a quick bite or a long evening, the experience is built around the idea that the best moments happen when they're shared.

We don't set out to recreate traditional dishes or follow a single cuisine. Instead we let the flavours, techniques and traditions that have shaped us meet in unexpected ways. As Head Chef Rafail Papimitoglou puts it, "It's a conversation between cultures."

Lebanese spices might find their way into Chinese-style dumplings. Japanese techniques refine Middle Eastern ingredients. Greek influences sit alongside flavours from Singapore and beyond. Familiar enough to feel comforting, yet layered enough to spark curiosity.

We cannot get enough of the hand-folded Shish Barak Dumplings: Lebanese seven-spiced meat wrapped in delicate Chinese-style dumpling dough. And the Devon Crab Brioche: fresh crab from East London's Fin & Flounder on crispy brioche, with smoked Napa cabbage, turnip and home-made Gochujang aioli.

Have an idea for the table? If you're a chef, cook, supper club, producer or food creative with an idea to share, from guest takeovers to collaborative dinners, we'd love to explore it. Drop us a line at rafail@madcats.uk.

Mischief, Well Poured

DRAG·01 / 05

RESERVE DRINKS MENU

Mad Cats was never meant to be just a restaurant or just a bar. From day one, the idea was a neighbourhood space where food, music and hospitality come together naturally, and the bar plays an equal role in that experience.

Whether you're joining us for a cocktail, a bottle of wine, a glass of sake or a full evening around the table, the aim is the same: a place where people can settle in, connect and stay a little longer than planned. As a hi-fi bar, music sits at the heart of what we do. Great drinks, great records and good company have a way of bringing people together.

Our drinks programme follows the same philosophy as the kitchen: a conversation between cultures, ingredients and traditions. Led by Bar Manager Khristopha McLaren and his team, the menu is rooted in seasonality, classic techniques and a spirit of experimentation. Influenced by his Cornish roots, Khris looks first to the ingredients growing around us before pairing them with flavours, spices and traditions from further afield.

Jungle Soul: a playful take on a classic tiki serve, combining tequila and gin infused with garam masala, house-made mango purée and pineapple. Lebanese Dirty Martini: gin, arak and za'atar. Bold, savoury and delicately aromatic in equal measure.

Great bars grow through collaboration. We've welcomed some of the UK's most exciting bars behind our counter: Brain Jar, Lab 22, Slow Pour, with Mother Mercy joining us next. Fancy taking over our bar? Get in touch at Khris@madcats.uk.

The Sound of Discovery

DRAG·01 / 04

RESERVE CALENDAR

The space houses 22 bespoke speakers, all tuned for an intimate, enveloping sound experience. The centrepiece is a Bliesma 6-inch mid-range dome: a rare piece tuned to handle the core frequencies of 240Hz to 4000Hz with unparalleled clarity. Active and dual passive radiators extend the low end, while a ribbon tweeter crowns the ensemble: crisp, airy and forward without being sharp.

Music Manager Sean Gorham puts as much love into the soundtrack as the guest-artist line-up. The nightly mood spans Japanese jazz and Arabic soul to breezy bossa nova and beyond. "It's about curation, not just putting vinyl on a shelf. It's discovery, ritual, emotion," says Sean. Mad Cats seamlessly transforms from an intimate dinner spot into a vinyl-fuelled bar as the night progresses.

Every Sunday, Mad Cats reconfigures its layout to welcome a live ensemble, with performances running from 4pm to 6pm. The setup is intentionally intimate, bringing musicians and guests into closer proximity. Brazilian music has become an increasingly important part of our Sunday sessions: warm, vibrant and distinctly Mad Cats.

Lightnin' Hopkins: the Texas blues master. Timeless, intimate and effortlessly human. A Tribe Called Quest: pioneers of jazz-infused hip hop, their laid-back grooves bring a different energy to the room.

Mad Cats is built on discovery and exchange. We're always interested in connecting with selectors, musicians, labels, promoters and creative collaborators. Have something to share with the room? Reach out to sean@madcats.uk.
